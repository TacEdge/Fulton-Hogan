The NZTA / IDM world
NZTA
NZ Transport Agency Waka Kotahi — the client. Owns the state highway network, now drives the asset management strategy directly under IDM. Also called "the Principal" in contract language.
Waka Kotahi is used equally. Both are fine.
IDM
Integrated Delivery Model — the new NZTA operating model from May 2026. NZTA owns asset management; contractors deliver the work. Replaces the old NOC model.
IDM = the model. IDC = the specific contract.
IDC
Integrated Delivery Contract — the physical contract. FH holds 7 of the 17 IDCs nationwide, 10-year terms. The largest single holder of IDM delivery risk in NZ.
NOC
Network Outcomes Contract — what IDC replaced. The old model where contractors had more autonomy over asset management. Gone from May 2026.
FH has been here before — the NOC was their world for years.
AFWP
Annual Forward Works Programme — the baseline programme of work on a network for the season. What TacEdge tracks delivery against.
Produced by NZTA under IDM; contractor delivers to it.
MSQA
Management, Surveillance & Quality Assurance — professional services providers who ride alongside the IDC contractor, checking quality on NZTA's behalf. Third party in the room.
Not FH. They are NZTA's eyes on FH.
Directory
supplier
The contestable pool — pre-qualified suppliers who compete for up to 20% of the work on each IDC network. FH coordinates with them; they don't report to FH.
Multi-party coordination problem. TacEdge's turf.
SH
State Highway — e.g. SH73 Arthur's Pass, SH6 West Coast. NZTA owns these. Local roads are the council's problem.
IDC covers state highways only.
The site / field world
STMS
Site Traffic Management Specialist — the certified person responsible for TM at a worksite. Required on site before traffic management can operate. Gated competency — you can't run a closure without one.
STMS-U = urban (higher speed); STMS-NP = non-primary. Different licences.
TMO
Traffic Management Operative — previously called Traffic Controller (TC). The person on the cones/paddle. Certified, not the same as STMS.
TMP
Traffic Management Plan — the approved plan for how traffic is managed at a worksite. Must be STMS-drafted, approved before work starts. Every work zone has one.
TMP expiry is an operational risk TacEdge tracks.
CoPTTM
Code of Practice for Temporary Traffic Management — the national rulebook all TMPs must comply with. NZTA document.
Pronounced "cop-tem." Everyone in the room will know it.
TTM
Temporary Traffic Management — the broader activity: cones, signs, stop/go, closures. TMP is the plan; TTM is the work.
JSA / SWMS
Job Safety Analysis / Safe Work Method Statement — the site safety documents crews sign on to each day. Gate for starting work in TacEdge Geotech; same principle applies here.
Maximo
IBM's asset management platform — FH's system of record for work orders, assets and maintenance history. The source TacEdge reads for programme state.
Do not suggest replacing it. "Reads from Maximo" is the line.
M-Field
Fulton Hogan's mobile field app — built in-house, used for plant prestarts and field reporting. One of Amanda's "one app, one task" apps.
Same caution: don't replace it. TacEdge sits above it.
ArcGIS
Esri's GIS platform — FH's spatial/asset layer. Where network assets live geographically. TacEdge overlays on top of it, reads from it.
SITREP
Situation Report — the formal update during or after an event. What TacEdge drafts and the controller approves before it goes to NZTA.
Military origin. Emergency managers use it daily.

What not to say
Don't say "digitise your field data." She solved that in 2013. The Hunter Civil framing ("field data becomes project overhead") is wrong for this room.
Don't quote geotech pricing ($1,500/month tiers). This is a national civil contractor. Commercial model is for after the pilot.
Don't imply live Maximo/ArcGIS integrations are built. Say clearly: pilot phase one proves the picture's value, phase two wires the APIs that earn it.
Don't let it become a build-vs-buy debate. If it goes there, use the neutral-ground answer and move on. Don't linger. She'll respect a clean answer over a drawn-out argument.
Don't make commitments. This is a scoping conversation. Observations, questions, and a small pilot ask is the right scope. Jonny, Mike and the DT resolution all come before any dev spend.
